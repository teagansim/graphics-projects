## What this is
In this project, textures were mapped onto spheres to create a semblance of a planet. Additionally, the size of the sphere is scaled up or down to make the planets sized relative to one another.This allows us to compare the size and texture of most of the planets in our solar system. There's also a light that rotates around, lighting up the texture on just one side at a time. The final result looks like a sped-up day breaking over a planet before night falls once again.

In total, there are options for (E)arth, (M)oon, (V)enus, (P)luto, (S)aturn, (N)eptune, (J)upiter, and (U)ranus. You can also toggle through the (T)exture, to turn it off, on, or on-with-lighting.

To run these projects, copy all files (except for the About.md) from the project folder to the downloaded GraphicsTemplateCode. Replace any files if prompted. Open the solution, build (or rebuild with "Rebuild Solution", as necessary), and run as normal.
